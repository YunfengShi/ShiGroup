Research

Atomic-scale mechanisms, macroscopic consequences

Our overarching goal is to use advanced molecular simulation to understand complex materials behavior at the atomic level. We then use that understanding to design new synthesis routes, microstructures and devices.

Toolbox

How we work

Molecular dynamics

Large-scale and accelerated MD of deformation, fracture, wear, growth and transport.

In-silico synthesis

Simulated casting, quenching and consolidation, so that model materials carry realistic processing histories.

Reactive force fields

Minimalist two-body reactive potentials that we develop in-house for polymers, oxides and perovskites.

ML & quantum optimization

Surrogate models and quantum algorithms for searching combinatorial design spaces.
