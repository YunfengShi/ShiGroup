Teaching

Fundamentals, told through their history

Prof. Shi's teaching centers on the core ideas of each subject, reinforced by the story of how they were discovered and by current research. The original experiment is often a better teacher than the textbook version.

Innovation

Interactive learning

Prof. Shi has built interactive Mathematica modules for diffusion and introductory materials science, so students can explore full time- and space-dependent solutions rather than static plots. Most labs ask students to change at most one line of code.

In spring 2020 he led the development of an individualized online testing system in which each student receives different question orders, numerical values and diagrams. It was used across all sections of ENGR 1600 and later adapted for other courses.
